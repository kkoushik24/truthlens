## ⚠️ Mandatory Honesty Disclosures

1. **Synthetic-Voice Classifier Disabled After Sanity Check Failure**:
   The neural classifier `MelodyMachine/Deepfake-audio-detection-V2` failed an empirical sanity check: it returned `fake=0.000017` for all 8 distinct real and synthetic speech clips in `samples/audio_eval/` (unchanged by volume scaling, noise, or trimming) while calling a pure 440 Hz mathematical sine tone 99.8% fake. It is completely insensitive to speech features and is disabled in scoring. The model name remains in `core/config.py` for future pluggable replacements.

2. **Audio Heuristics Are UNVALIDATED on Real Speech**:
   The deterministic heuristics (high-frequency cutoff, spectral flatness/noise floor consistency, pitch variation/prosody, and silence/breath regularity) are **UNVALIDATED ON REAL SPEECH**. Compression, noise reduction, studio recording, phone microphones, and text-to-speech readers can produce identical acoustic characteristics.

3. **8-Clip Evaluation Benchmark Disclaimer**:
   The 8-clip evaluation (`samples/audio_eval/labels.csv`) is a small preliminary sanity set created to detect catastrophic model failures. It is **NOT a valid statistical accuracy claim** for the heuristics.
